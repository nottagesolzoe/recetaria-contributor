Copy the RecetArIA prompt and paste it into a chat where you solved a research problem with AI. Send the message.

The chat will suggest possible recipes. Choose “Create recipe”, “Discard” or “Not transferable”. Your selected recipe will appear in the same chat.

You do not need to install anything, download a folder or know how to code.

Recipes are reviewed and tested before editorial approval. Creating a recipe does not authorize submission. Sending to Drive requires an enabled connection; the prompt alone does not create one.
